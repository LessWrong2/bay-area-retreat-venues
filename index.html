About this list

Conference venues for roughly 60 people, Friday Oct 16 – Sunday Oct 18, 2026, in two lists. Drive from SF is the original Northern California set, compiled Aug 19, 2026. Fly-in West was added Aug 27, 2026: thirteen venues across the US West chosen for looks first, then for being close to an airport with a year-round nonstop from SFO or OAK. Capacities and phone numbers should be verified at time of booking.

Tier A
Strongest matches to the Lighthaven / SSS Ranch / The Shadows profile: real character, whole-site exclusive use, on-site lodging near 60 — within about 2.5 hours of SF for the drive list, or within about half an hour of a nonstop airport for the fly-in list.
Tier B
Solid and bookable, but each trades something: aesthetics, distance, guest-type restrictions, or dorm-style lodging.
Tier C
Worth a call for a specific reason (architecture, hot springs, all-inclusive ease, in-city convenience), with a clear tradeoff.

The fly-in West list

Airport drive times were computed with OSRM from the terminal to the property's own coordinates, not quoted from marketing copy. Every airport used here has a year-round nonstop from SFO or OAK, checked against the current route tables: PSP, TUS, PHX, ABQ, RDM, SEA, PDX, SBA and SBP. Seasonal-only routes (Jackson Hole, Missoula, Aspen, Bishop) were excluded because mid-October falls in their gaps.

Six venues are inside 20 minutes of the gate: Cedarbrook (8 min), Ace Palm Springs (8), Sycamore Mineral Springs (17, with a free group shuttle), Two Bunch Palms (18), The Hermosa Inn (19) and Edgefield (20). Brasada, Mountain Shadows and El Capitan Canyon sit at 20–22 minutes. Three run 30–33 minutes — Tanque Verde, Hacienda del Sol and FivePine — which needs a chartered coach for 60 people, but each is a single straight road from the terminal.

Timing

Oct 16–18 is a Friday-to-Sunday in peak Northern California wedding and harvest season. Napa, Sonoma and Santa Cruz Mountains venues book that weekend 9–15 months out, so call rather than email and ask about the date first.

Capacity

“Sleeps on-site” is the binding constraint at 60 people. Westerbeke (40–52), Wilbur (~40–50), Land of Medicine Buddha (56) and Hidden Villa (39) all need either doubling up, camping, or nearby overflow lodging.

Pricing found

Westerbeke: $255–270 per person per night on weekends, plus 14% occupancy and 9.25% sales tax; $135 per person for a day conference. St. Dorothy's Rest 2026: $135 single / $105 double / $77–97 dorm per person per night, plus $22 per meal. Hidden Villa day spaces: $300–$850. Everyone else quotes on request.

Restrictions

Bishop's Ranch hosts nonprofit groups only. Vallombrosa takes religious, educational or charitable groups only. Camp Navarro will not answer booking phone calls — use their form. Westminster Woods only takes Friday–Sunday bookings during the school year, which happens to be this exact window.
